Because the plan is a 12-month plan. Pricing fixes can land in weeks, while team, systems and lead flow compound over the year. Most members stay for 3+ years.

We look at your margins, capacity and growth constraints before recommending anything. Most members find that pricing corrections fund the wider work.

The free trainings and Greg's book cover the fundamentals. The Mastermind is for a bespoke plan, advanced training, implementation, accountability and community.

No. The method is consistent, but the planning session tailors the next 12 months to your business, numbers and biggest constraints.
