The programme runs on the DEVELOP method. Every goal, review and training maps to one of these five.

  1. 01 Plan The 12-month plan, and the numbers that prove you're on it.
  2. 02 Attract Lead flow you control, instead of one architect's goodwill.
  3. 03 Convert Pricing and follow-up that win work at proper margins.
  4. 04 Deliver Job costing and site systems that protect the margin you quoted.

07 / Investment

What it costs, and what moves the price

The Mastermind is a single investment, paid monthly or annually, with a 12-month minimum commitment. We go through the exact figure openly on the Scale Session once we know the size and shape of your business, so you can judge it against your own margins rather than a number on a page.

Why a 12-month commitment?

Because the plan is a 12-month plan. Pricing fixes land in weeks; team, systems and lead flow compound over the year. Most members stay for 3+ years.

What determines whether it's worth it?

Margin, mostly. On a £1 million turnover, a 3 point margin improvement covers the year's fee. Most members find the pricing correction alone does that in the first quarter.

Is there a cheaper way to start?

The free trainings and the book cover the basic method. The Mastermind is for bespoke strategies, advanced training, implementing it with coaching, accountability and the community.

Do prices vary?

The programme is one price, and it decreases with loyalty pricing in years 2 and 3. What varies is where you start: the planning session tailors the 12 months to your business, not a generic curriculum.
