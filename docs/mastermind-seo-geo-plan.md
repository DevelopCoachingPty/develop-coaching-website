## Pillar 1: Plan

Hub: How to build a 12-month construction business plan.

Supporting articles: the numbers every construction business plan needs; profit versus turnover for growing builders; and a practical monthly management review. Link each article to the hub, adjacent pillars and the Mastermind page.

## Pillar 2: Attract

Hub: How established builders create a controlled lead pipeline.

Supporting articles: building architect relationships without relying on one referrer; reducing referral risk in a construction company; and tracking enquiries from first contact to qualified opportunity. Keep claims practical and avoid promising lead volumes.

## Pillar 3: Convert

Hub: How to price and follow up construction work at proper margins.

Supporting articles: estimating discipline before a quote is sent; a construction quote follow-up process; and measuring conversion quality rather than win rate alone. Separate quoted margin, expected margin and realised margin.

## Pillar 4: Deliver

Hub: How builders protect quoted margin during project delivery.

Supporting articles: live job costing for growing builders; cost-to-complete reviews before margin drifts; and a site-to-office handover checklist. Do not present software capability or financial outcomes without current product evidence.

## Pillar 5: Scale

Hub: How to structure and lead a growing construction company.

Supporting articles: defining roles and accountability; when to hire senior operational support; and weekly leadership rhythms that reduce owner dependence. Avoid invented credentials or universal staffing ratios.

## Publishing rules

Each hub should define the problem, give a practical framework, answer commercial-intent questions and link clearly to the existing Scale Session. Supporting articles should answer one narrow question and feed authority back to their hub. Use only verified member proof, qualify time-bound results and preserve the exact meaning of approved testimonial claims.

Final titles and publication order must be checked against Search Console query data before commissioning. No search volumes, rankings or traffic forecasts have been invented in this plan.
