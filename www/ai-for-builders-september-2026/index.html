For Owners Building A Company, Not Another Job For Themselves.

This is for you if

  • Your UK building company turns over £1m or more.
  • You already have a team and office support.
  • You want larger, more desirable projects.
  • You care about reliable margins and professional delivery.
  • You want fewer questions landing on your desk.

This is not for you if

  • You want beginner prompts or a tour of model names.
  • You expect AI to make unchecked commercial decisions.
  • You want a magic replacement for a capable team.
  • You only want more leads, regardless of fit or margin.
  • You run every job alone and have no team to coordinate.

No. AI can research, compare, organise and prepare. Your team remains responsible for commercial and delivery decisions.
