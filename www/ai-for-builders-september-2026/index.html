What you leave with

Six ways to make the next ideal project easier to win and safer to deliver.

The workshop follows the work in the order your team actually handles it.

01 | Convert

Read the room after the meeting

Pull out what the client cares about, what worries them, who decides and which questions still need an answer.

02 | Convert

Build a presentation around this client

Use their priorities, your relevant project photos and the right case studies. No generic claims about quality.

03 | Plan

Compare the real materials cost

Check specification, quantities, delivery and lead time before a cheap headline price becomes an expensive mistake.

04 | Deliver

Find the gaps in subcontractor quotes

Compare each quote against the same scope, spot exclusions and prepare clarification questions for approval.

05 | Deliver

Hand the project over properly

Give the project manager the agreed scope, budget, outstanding decisions and a clear financial record.

06 | Scale

Take repeatable work off your desk

Prepare weekly client updates, record an office process and request progress from your phone without removing sensible approval checks.
