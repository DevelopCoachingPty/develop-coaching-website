From protection to delivery

Winning the job is only the start.

In February we covered protection: site, payment, adjudication. How to stop a job going wrong.

This time we're moving on to delivery. How you keep the margin once the contract is signed. How you share the pressure when the workload gets heavy.

Then you'll hear from members who are further ahead. What actually helped them. What changed as the business grew.

Margin survives through delivery, not just winning.
The agenda

Three sessions on delivery, leadership and growth.

We keep it practical. No fluff. Just tools and conversations you can use on site and in the office.

09:15 · 60 minutes

Pricing to Deliver a Project, Not Just Win the Tender

Where margin actually leaks: allowances, variations, and the QS review that should happen before you quote. Control the number all the way through delivery, not just on the day you win.

Leave with: a way to protect the margin after the contract is signed.

10:30 · 45 minutes

Sharing the Load

Practical tools for handling the stress and workload of a tough industry. Not a lecture. Tactics that work, and how to use the people in this room instead of carrying it alone.

Leave with: tools you'll actually use on a bad week.

11:15 · 60 minutes

Member Panel

Members further down the road on their real challenges, their wins, and what has given them the most value: the tools, the coaching and the community.

Leave with: a clearer picture of what changes as your business scales.
