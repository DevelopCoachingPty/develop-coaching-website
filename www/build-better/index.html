That covers everything: the coaching, the one-to-ones, the messaging, the live events and all the software. There's nothing extra to buy later.

The first month's the busiest, with a one-to-one every week. After that it's an hour to an hour and a half a week, working on the business. Monday morning or Friday afternoon, whatever suits you.

Yes. Twelve months gives your plan time to work, and the changes time to bed in.
