I'm flat out. Where do I find the time?

Being flat out is the problem we're here to fix. A quiet month to sort the business out never comes.

The first month's the busiest, with a one-to-one every week. After that, plan on an hour to an hour and a half a week, working on the business. Monday morning or Friday afternoon, whatever suits you.

Your plan is broken down into weekly actions, so that time goes on the next thing that matters, not on working out where to start.

If it looks right, you book a Scale Session. It's a free 15-minute call, and we both get a clear picture of which pillar is holding you back.

If it isn't right for you yet, we send you our free training.

Who will I be working with?

Me and my team. I coach members myself, alongside five other coaches, and every one of us works only with construction companies.

There's quantity surveying and finance on the team. So when you ask about pricing a job, your margin or your cash, the answer comes from someone who knows the trade.

As for me, I've been in construction for 30 years. I was on the tools at twenty, then ran companies turning over millions. One failed, and I rebuilt knowing my numbers every month.

I've already got an accountant. How is this different?

Keep your accountant. Year-end accounts tell you how last year went, long after those jobs have finished.

We work on this week. Which jobs to go after. What each job is costing against what you quoted. How and when you get paid. Who does what, so it doesn't all go through you.

It isn't a consultant's report either. You get the plan, the software to run it, and a coach holding you to it every week.
