Why isn't the price on this page, and how much is it?

I know it's frustrating not seeing a figure up front. We cover the cost on the second call because putting a price out before we've looked at your business turns it into guesswork, and we want to be sure we can actually help you first. Your membership covers the coaching, all six software tools and the three live events a year, so you know everything that's in it. You'll have the exact figure in front of you on that second call before you decide anything.

I don't want an £8m company, I just want more profit and my evenings back. Is this for my size and trade?

You don't have to want an £8m firm to be here. Most builders join wanting proper profit, better jobs and their evenings back first, not just more turnover and more headaches. Our members are a whole range of builders and trade contractors, and you'll see plenty of them on this page. Richard J joined at around £750k and is now at about £1.1m, and James and Kate fixed a £1.9m business running at a loss in 2022 before growing past £8m in 2026. I've paid a business coach before and got nothing out of it. Why would this be different?

Sam and Nathan, two electrical contractors on this page, felt the same way after a coach who, in their words, "promised the earth and didn't really deliver". I'm flat out as it is. Where am I meant to find the time?

Being flat out is the exact problem we're here to fix, because a quiet month to sort the business out never comes. Your first month is the busiest, with a one-to-one with a coach each week to get your goals, numbers and software moving. After that, allow about an hour to an hour and a half a week working on the business, whether that's Monday morning or Friday afternoon. Your plan is broken down into weekly actions, so that time goes straight onto the right next thing instead of wondering where to start.

Am I locked in for twelve months straight away?

The full programme is twelve months, because fixing margins, pricing and delegation takes time to bed in and stops good habits getting dropped on the first busy month. If you're unsure, there is a paid 30-day love it or leave it option so you can test the programme before committing to the year. It's like taking on a new subbie: you try them on one job, you pay them for it, and you watch how they perform before deciding. We'll go through how the 30 days work and what they cost on the call.

Do I really need six pieces of software, and what if I'm no good with tech?

You don't need to use all six on day one, and they aren't there to pad out the offer. All six tools are included in your membership because each one handles a part of the business you're probably still running on spreadsheets or scraps of paper, from finding architect work to job costing and health and safety. You don't need to be good with software either, because we set them up with you so you're comfortable with them. Start with one tool on one live job, and bring the rest in only when you're ready.

Who will actually be coaching me? Do they know construction?

Me and my team of construction coaches. I coach members myself alongside five other coaches, and every one of us works only with building and trade companies. So when you ask about pricing a job, your margin or your cash, the answer comes from someone who knows the trade.
