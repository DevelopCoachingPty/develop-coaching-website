  4. Jarvis and Your Google Account Data

    Jarvis (https://jarvis.develop-coaching.com) is an AI assistant provided by Develop Coaching Pty Ltd to our members. This section explains how Jarvis handles data from your Google Account. It applies in addition to the rest of this Privacy Policy.

    What Jarvis connects to

    Connecting your Google Account to Jarvis is entirely optional. If you choose to connect it, Jarvis asks your permission through Google’s own consent screen before it can access anything, and you can see exactly which permissions you are granting. Depending on the permissions you approve, Jarvis may access:

    – Gmail: reading your messages, sending messages on your behalf, and organising your mailbox (for example applying labels, archiving, or marking as read).
    – Google Calendar: viewing and managing your events.
    – Google Drive: viewing and managing files you ask Jarvis to work with.
    – Google Docs and Google Sheets: reading and editing documents and spreadsheets you ask Jarvis to work with.
    – Google Contacts: read-only access to your contacts, so Jarvis can address messages and invitations to the right people.

    Why Jarvis needs this access

    Jarvis only uses this access to carry out the tasks you ask it to perform. For example, summarising your inbox, drafting or sending a reply you have asked for, finding a time in your calendar and booking a meeting, or creating and updating a document or spreadsheet on your instruction. Access is always tied to a request you have made.

    How your data is used and stored

    – Your Google data is used solely to provide the Jarvis features described above to you.
    – Each member’s Jarvis assistant runs in its own separate environment. Your Google data is not shared with, or made visible to, other members.
    – We store the access credentials issued by Google (access and refresh tokens) securely so that Jarvis can continue working on your behalf without asking you to sign in repeatedly. These credentials are encrypted and access to them is restricted.
    – We retain the content of your Google data only for as long as needed to complete the task you asked for and to maintain the working memory of your assistant. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

    What we do not do

    – We do not sell your Google data.
    – We do not use your Google data for advertising, or transfer it to others for advertising purposes.
    – We do not use your Google data to train, or improve, generalised artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
    – We do not allow humans to read your Google data, except with your explicit permission (for example when you ask us to investigate a problem), where it is necessary for security purposes such as investigating abuse, or where we are required to do so by law.

    Third parties involved

    To generate its responses, Jarvis sends the information relevant to your request to the AI model provider that powers your assistant. These providers act on our instructions, process the data only to return a response, and do not use it to train their models. We do not share your Google data with any other third party.

    Limited Use

    Jarvis’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy

    Removing access

    You can disconnect your Google Account from Jarvis at any time in the Jarvis dashboard, or by removing our access directly in your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When you disconnect, we revoke the credentials Google issued to us and delete the stored tokens. To request deletion of any Google data we still hold, contact us at greg@develop-coaching.com.
