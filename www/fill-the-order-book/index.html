The three blocks

Every quote from the last 18 months that was never won and never formally lost is still live. We count them, name the biggest ten, and send each one a single plain nine-word question. You'll send the first one on the call.

You'll also leave knowing what to say to each kind of reply: too dear, not yet, weren't sure, went elsewhere.

Why now

Every autumn I watch the same thing happen with the builders I work with. Clients go quiet in November. The office winds down before Christmas. In January the client finally decides, and the job starts in May.

The builders who are busy in January did their booking in October and November. If you leave this until the planner is already empty, you'll be pricing against everyone else who left it too.
