The Five Leaks We Plug

Same five in nearly every building business I look at. We go through each one, what it costs, and exactly how to close it.

1

Markup Is Not Margin

You think you're adding 20%. You're not. One bad calculation repeated across a year's worth of jobs quietly wipes out tens of thousands, and the quote looked fine when it went out.

2

Prelims Nobody Priced

Scaffolding, skips, welfare, the site van. The bits that never make it onto the quote but always make it onto the invoice. Across your projects that's £50k to £100k a year.

3

Jobs That Overrun

Every week a job runs past its date costs you twice. Once in the cost of being there, and again in the job you could have started instead.

4

The Goodwill Trap

One missing clause and you carry the cost when the programme slips. You're working for free and the contract you signed says that's fine.

5

Variations Done For Free

Priced late, priced low, or never invoiced at all. The client gets the extra, you get nothing, and nobody notices until the final account.

Add them up across a year and it's six figures for most builders turning over £1M or more. Fixing one of the five can pay for the workshop several hundred times over.
