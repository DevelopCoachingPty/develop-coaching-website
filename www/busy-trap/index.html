Straight answers

Who is it for?

Building and trade company owners turning over roughly £750k to £5m, with an office team of two to ten plus trades. The work's coming in, but the margins don't match the effort, and too much still depends on you.

Who isn't it for?

Sole traders without a team, start-ups with no trading history, companies selling services to builders, and anyone looking for a quick fix.

How much time does it take?

The first month's the busiest, with a one-to-one every week. After that it's an hour to an hour and a half a week, on the business. Monday morning, Friday afternoon, whatever suits you.

How much is it?

We go through the cost on your second call, once we've looked at your business and what it needs. You can pay monthly or annually. And there's nothing extra to buy later. The plan, the coaching, the community, the live events and the software are the programme.

Is it really twelve months?

Yes. It's a twelve-month commitment. It gives your plan room to work, and it stops good changes being dropped the first busy month.

But if you're unsure, there's a paid thirty-day love it or leave it. You test out the programme first. Then you make an informed decision. Thirty days in, you've been through your own numbers with a coach. The first leak often shows up in that first month. You're deciding on what you've seen, not on what you've been sold.

Love it, and you commit to the twelve months knowing what you're in for. Leave it, and you leave. But once you've committed, you're in for the year. We'll go through how the thirty days work, and what they cost, on the call.

30-day love it or leave it The details of the 30-day option are covered on your call.
What support do I get between calls?

A messaging app you can use any time, day or night, and a group WhatsApp with the other members.

I'm not good with software.

You don't need to be. We set your tools up with you on your first one-to-one, and you don't have to move the whole firm across in a week. Start with one job. If you get stuck, you message us and we'll sort it.

Do you promise results?

No. Nobody honest can. What we do promise is a plan built around your business, and the support to carry it out.
