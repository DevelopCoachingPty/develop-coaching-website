Built for established operators

You have built a good business. Now it needs to become a better one.

The work is coming in, but the margins do not match the effort. Important decisions still wait for you, and too much of the company lives in your head.

From the outside, it looks successful. Inside, you are still checking every quote, solving every site problem and carrying the pressure home. A busier diary has created more responsibility, but not the freedom you expected when you started the business.

  • You cannot switch off because the team still needs your answer
  • Turnover is growing faster than cash and profit
  • You know what needs fixing, but urgent work keeps winning
  • Another year like this will make the business bigger, not better

This is for you if...

  • You run a building or trade company turning over roughly £750k to £5 million
  • You have an office team of 2 to 10 people, plus trade support
  • You want more reliable margins from the work already coming in
  • Too many systems and decisions still depend on you
  • You are ready to replace daily firefighting with structured growth
  • You will spend 1 to 1.5 hours a week working on the business

It is not for...

  • Sole traders without a team to structure
  • Start-ups with no trading history
  • Anyone looking for a silver bullet
  • Companies selling services to builders

The investment

A serious programme for a serious growth plan.

The Mastermind is paid monthly or annually with a 12-month minimum commitment. We explain the exact figure openly on the Scale Session once we understand the size and shape of your business.

For context A 3-point margin improvement on £1m turnover covers the year's fee.
Talk through the numbers
Why a 12-month commitment?

Because the plan is a 12-month plan. Pricing fixes can land in weeks, while team, systems and lead flow compound over the year. Most members stay for 3+ years.

How do I know it will be worth it?

We look at your margins, capacity and growth constraints before recommending anything. Most members find that pricing corrections fund the wider work.

Is there a cheaper way to start?

The free trainings and Greg's book cover the fundamentals. The Mastermind is for a bespoke plan, advanced training, implementation, accountability and community.

Does everyone follow the same curriculum?

No. The method is consistent, but the planning session tailors the next 12 months to your business, numbers and biggest constraints.
